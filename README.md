# About the Game

You are a hardworking beaver facing the ultimate survival challenge. Deep in the wilderness, the river is rising, and your home is threatened.

Your main goal is simple yet demanding: **keep the dam intact, manage your daily resource gathering, and survive until the timer runs out.**

Built under intense time constraints, this game focuses on **core survival mechanics, tight resource loops, and constant pressure against the elements.**

## Key Features

### Top-Down Survival & Exploration
Navigate the map, dodge obstacles, and find crucial **wood and stone nodes** to fuel your repairs.

### The Dam Integrity System
Watch the water levels closely. The river current constantly erodes the dam, forcing you to balance exploration with urgent maintenance.

### Resource Management
Collect materials with your limited personal inventory, store them safely at your lodge, and use them on-site to keep everything afloat.

### Beat the Clock
Survive through the tense countdown timer where every second counts to prevent a total disaster.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `WASD` | Move your beaver around the map |
| `E` | Interact with resource nodes, storage, and repair sites |
